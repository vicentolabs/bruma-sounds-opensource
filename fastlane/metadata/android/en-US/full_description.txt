Bruma plays ambient background sounds alongside music in other Android apps.
Mix several tracks and adjust the volume of each layer.

It began with a personal need for a simple, free, ad-free way to play
background sounds with music. The source is shared so anyone can use it,
study it, and contribute.

The interface is available in Spanish, English, German, Simplified Chinese,
and Russian. It follows the device language; Android 13 and newer also let
you choose Bruma's language in system settings.

Includes 21 offline recordings: rain, storms, fire, river, ocean, wind, fans,
ambient sounds, and colored noise.

Features include favorites, category filters, quick scenes, notification
controls, and a timer with gradual fade-out.

No Internet connection is needed for playback. Simultaneous playback with
other apps may vary by device and Android version.
