Bruma plays ambient sounds in the background alongside music in other Android
apps. Mix several tracks and adjust each layer's volume independently.

It began with a search for a simple, free, ad-free way to play background
sounds with music. The source is shared so anyone can use it, study it, and
contribute.

Includes 21 offline recordings: rain, storms, fire, river, ocean, wind, fans,
ambient sounds, and colored noise.

Features include favorites, category filters, quick scenes, notification
controls, and a timer with gradual fade-out.

No Internet connection is needed for playback. Simultaneous playback with
other apps may vary by device and Android version.

The source code is MIT-licensed. Recordings are CC0; sources are listed in
AUDIO_CREDITS.md.

The app interface is currently in Spanish.
